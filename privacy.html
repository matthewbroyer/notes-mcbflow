Privacy

Effective 2026-10-16

notes.mcbflow.com is a free notes app that runs in your browser. You don't need an account: everything works and saves on your device. If you choose to, you can sign in with a free mcbflow.com account to sync your notes across your devices. There are no analytics, ads or tracking scripts.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter is saved in your browser's storage on this device. Unless you sign in to sync, it isn't sent to us, and we can't see it. It isn't encrypted, so anyone who can use this browser profile can read it; avoid storing passwords or other secrets in notes.

  • notes: your notes, checklists, tags, colors, and the name, type and size of any files you attach
  • notes.files (browser database): the files you attach
  • notes.sync: sync progress, only if you sign in to sync
  • notes.keepSignedIn: set only if you turn off "Keep me signed in on this device", so the sign-in ends when the browser closes
  • notes.handles (browser database): in Chrome and Edge, which backup file "Save backup now" updates (the browser's own reference to the file you chose, not a copy of it)
  • notes.draft: a note you were still writing when you left the page, so it can be restored; removed once it's saved
  • notes.theme: whether you chose light or dark mode
  • notes.hasVisited: remembers that you've seen the welcome screen and that the sample notes were already added, so neither comes back
  • notes.lastBackup: the date you last saved or exported a backup, shown in the Data menu

Clearing your browser's site data removes it from this device. The app's own delete option (Data → Clear all data) removes your notes and attachments too, and also deletes your synced notes if you're signed in. If you install the app, your browser also keeps a copy of the app's files (not your data) so it opens offline.

What leaves your device

  • Nothing, unless you sign in to sync (below). Links inside your notes only open when you click them, in a new tab.

Optional sync

If you sign in with a free mcbflow.com account (the account button in the top bar, Settings → Account & sync, or Data → Sync across devices), your notes, checklists, tags, colors, pins, archive and Trash, and the name, type and size of attached files are copied to your account so your other devices match. The attached files themselves are not synced: they stay on the device you added them on, and other devices show them as "not on this device". Export a backup to move files. Your theme and unsaved draft stay on each device. The account database is run by Supabase (servers in the eastern United States); only your account can read your records. Signing out stops syncing and keeps the notes on this device. You can export or delete your synced data, or delete your account, on the mcbflow.com account page. More detail is in the mcbflow.com privacy policy.

Our web host receives the standard request information every website receives (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver the pages. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Exports and backups

Save backup now and Export All (JSON) save a .json file of your notes, including the attached files on this device, to your device. Export All (CSV) saves a spreadsheet of your notes, and Export All (ZIP) saves the backup, the spreadsheet and your attached files in one .zip. All of them are made on your device; where the files go after that is up to you. Import Backup and Import from Google Keep read files you choose (including a .zip) inside your browser; nothing is uploaded. Pictures and files you open in the app's viewer are shown from this device. Share images are drawn on your device and only go where you send them.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
