Tips

Tags
Type #idea anywhere in a note. Tags appear in the sidebar so you can filter by them.
Links between notes
Type [[Note title]] to link to another note. The linked note lists it under "Linked from".
Web links
Paste any https:// address and it becomes clickable.
Attachments
Attach files with the paperclip or paste a screenshot. Click a picture or file to open it right here in the app.
Keyboard
Esc or Ctrl+Enter saves and closes. Ctrl+B / I / U format text. Ctrl+S saves a backup. Start a line with • or 1. and Enter continues the list.
Can I install it like an app?
In Chrome or Edge, use Install below (or the install icon in the address bar). On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Backup & data

Import from Google Keep

  1. Go to takeout.google.com, click Deselect all, tick Keep only, and create the export.
  2. Download the .zip Google emails you.
  3. Choose that .zip here (or the .json files from its Takeout/Keep folder).

Keeps titles, text, checklists, colors, pins, archive and trash, labels (as #tags), links and dates. Notes you've already imported are skipped. Everything is read on your device; nothing is uploaded.

What’s new in Notes

1.5 · Oct 16, 2026

  • A new Settings page, laid out the same way in every mcbflow.com app.
  • An account button in the top bar, and “Keep me signed in on this device” for shared computers.
  • A short welcome screen on your first visit.
  • A clearer Data menu: Save backup now (Ctrl+S), and Export All as JSON, CSV or ZIP. The ZIP holds your backup, a spreadsheet and your attachments.
  • Pictures and attached files open right in the app instead of downloading.
  • Pick a profile picture and screen name for your mcbflow.com account (account button → Edit profile); every app shows the same one.
  • A greeting above All notes (“Good morning”, with your name when you’re signed in).
  • A proper gear icon for Settings, and a new name style: Notes ∙ mcbflow.com.

1.4 · Oct 15, 2026

  • Optional free mcbflow.com account to sync notes across your devices.
  • Attachments live in the browser’s database, so they no longer eat into the note space.
